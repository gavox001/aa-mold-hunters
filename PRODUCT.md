# AA Mold Hunters - PRODUCT.md

## Users

### Primary Users
- **South Florida homeowners** facing mold or water damage emergencies
- **Property managers** overseeing residential buildings in Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach counties
- **Insurance agents** seeking certified mold remediation partners

### User Profiles
| Profile | Need | Pain Point |
|---------|------|------------|
| Homeowner with water damage | Fast response, peace of mind | Mold spreads in 24-48h, unsure who to trust |
| Anxious parent | Health safety for family | Children/elderly vulnerable to mold spores |
| Property investor | Minimal downtime, documentation | Need receipts for insurance claims |
| Real estate agent | Fast closing, liability protection | Mold discovered before sale |

### Emergency Context
Users reach out in crisis: after a hurricane, a pipe burst, discovered black mold behind walls. They're scared, vulnerable to predatory companies, and need immediate reassurance that help exists.

---

## Product Purpose

Emergency mold inspection and remediation services for South Florida homeowners. The website must:
1. **Convince** visitors this is a legitimate, trustworthy company (not a scam)
2. **Reduce anxiety** through clear process explanation and credentials
3. **Drive immediate action** via phone call or contact form
4. **Convert insurance claims** by making claims process frictionless

---

## Brand Personality

### Tone of Voice
- **Authoritative but calm** — emergency experts who don't add to panic
- **Honest and transparent** — no hidden fees, clear pricing, realistic timelines
- **Emotionally intelligent** — acknowledges fear without exploiting it
- **Local expertise** — South Florida specific (humidity, hurricanes, older housing stock)

### Messaging Pillars
1. **Speed** — "24/7 Emergency Response", "Same-Day Service Available"
2. **Certification** — Licensed, insured, IICRC certified, EPA compliant
3. **Protection** — "Protect your home and family"
4. **Peace of mind** — Satisfaction guarantee, warranty on work

### Anti-References (what we're NOT)
- Alarmist fear-mongering about "toxic black mold"
- High-pressure sales tactics
- Vague pricing or "call for quote" avoidance
- Generic stock photo aesthetics

---

## Strategic Principles

1. **Trust signals everywhere** — certifications, licenses, testimonials, ratings visible above the fold
2. **Action-oriented CTAs** — every section has a clear next step (call, form, schedule)
3. **Process transparency** — 4-step process shown clearly to reduce uncertainty
4. **Mobile-first** — emergency happens on phones, forms must work on small screens
5. **Bilingual** — South Florida's demographics require EN/ES toggle
6. **Insurance-friendly** — documentation, itemized quotes, direct insurance communication
